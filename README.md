# <ins>AuditGuard: Risk and Compliance Insights</ins>

This project focuses on the Big 4 Auditing Firms namely **KPMG, PwC, Delloite and Ernst & Young** with an intent to advise a potential future investor on what sector (_industry_) they'd be best suited to go into based on analysis from 2020 to 2025. The sectors under study in the analysis namely were; **Tech, Retail, Finance and Healthcare**. Factors under study that would contribute towards the final decision would revolve around **Audit Effectiveness Scores, Client Satisfaction Scores, Revenue Impact and Compliance Breach Rates at Industry Level. 

In addition, the project assesses the influence of AI usage in Auditing various industry players by the Big 4 on Revenue and Audit Effectiveness. This would then help in determining what firm the investor(s) should hire for audting services once they settle on their industry of choice. 

To achieve the above analysis an Excel Dashboard, **AuditGuard**, was created based on Big 4 Auditing data, 2020 -2025 sourced from Kaggle. New Measures such as the _Compliance Breach Rate (CBR)_ and _Work Efficiency Ratio (WER)_ were computed to help in drilling into the intended analysis, conclusions and key recommendations. From the AuditGuard Excel Dashboard insights can be derived by Year, Auditing Firm, Workload Category, AI Usage or Industry Affected by potential investor(s) _(clients)_ directly while seeking their best fit industry for investment and a potential auditing firm once they begin day to day operations.

This project was done as per the requirements of Everything Data Africa _(Data Analytics Cohort 3, 2025)_ in collaboration with DataCamp.
